# NavaFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-19

This Privacy Policy explains how Tinesh Awai Veera, operating NavaFit ("NavaFit", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data when you:

- visit `https://navafit.sg`
- submit forms, enquiries, or waitlist information
- use NavaFit previews, tools, or content
- create or use an account in the NavaFit Alignment app
- connect supported sign-in providers, health services, or wearable integrations

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use NavaFit services.

## 3. Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on what you use, we may collect the following categories of data.

### A. Information you provide directly

This may include:

- full name
- preferred name or nickname
- email address
- contact number
- age
- occupation
- programme goals, fitness preferences, and written responses
- support requests or messages you send us

### B. Account and authentication data

If you create or use an account, we may collect:

- login email address
- authentication provider details
- profile identifiers
- session and login status information

Where available, sign-in may be provided through providers such as Apple, Google, or email/password authentication. Authentication services may also provide us with basic profile data such as your email address or display name.

### C. Wellness, training, and app usage data

If you use NavaFit app features, we may collect or generate:

- workout session records
- training notes
- training duration
- readiness, stamina, endurance, recovery, breath, and similar fitness or wellness indicators
- telemetry snapshots
- sync history and event logs
- app usage events and interaction logs

### D. Health and connected-device data

If you choose to connect health or wearable services, we may collect data made available through those services, which may include:

- wellness or fitness-related metrics
- workout or activity information
- connected-device identifiers
- sync timestamps and connection status

This may include data from services or platforms such as Apple sign-in, Apple Health, Apple Watch, Garmin, or other supported integrations when and if those integrations are enabled by you.

### E. Nutrition, assessment, and preview inputs

If you use preview tools or submit assessment information, we may collect data such as:

- sex at birth
- age
- weight
- height
- activity level
- stated goals
- training or nutrition preferences

### F. Camera and preview features

The current NavaFit site preview states that certain pose-preview features run locally in the browser. If you choose to grant camera access for a local preview, camera input may be processed on your device for that experience.

If future versions of NavaFit process camera data, images, video, or pose output on our servers or through third parties, we will update this Privacy Policy and request any required consent or permission before doing so.

### G. Technical and device information

We may collect technical information such as:

- browser type
- device type
- approximate location inferred from network or regional settings
- IP address
- pages or screens viewed
- timestamps
- error logs, diagnostics, and security-related events

## 4. How We Use Personal Data

We may use personal data to:

- operate and provide NavaFit services
- create and manage user accounts
- authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
- respond to enquiries and support requests
- manage early-bird, programme, or waitlist submissions
- personalize training, wellness, or recovery experiences

- improve service quality, safety, performance, and reliability
- detect abuse, fraud, errors, or unauthorized access

- communicate important updates about the service

Some NavaFit features may involve wellness, fitness, or health-related information, which can be sensitive in nature.

We will only collect or use this type of data:

- when you choose to provide it
- when you choose to connect a supported service or device

- where required, with your consent

NavaFit is not intended to replace medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.
